
# Given a formula and it's negation, deduce any formula.

let $\alpha$, $\beta$ be formula
    
$(\neg \alpha \rightarrow (\neg \beta \rightarrow \neg \alpha))$ ref{A1}

if $\neg \alpha$:

    $(\neg \beta \rightarrow \neg \alpha)$ ref{DT1, 6}

    $((\neg \beta \rightarrow \neg \alpha) \rightarrow (\alpha \rightarrow \beta))$ ref{A3}


    $(\alpha \rightarrow \beta)$ ref{MP, 10, 12}
    
$(\neg \alpha \rightarrow (\alpha \rightarrow \beta))$ ref{DT2, 15} label{result}
