
# Given a formula and its negation, any formula follows

with $\alpha$, $\beta$ as formula:
    
    $(\neg \alpha \rightarrow (\neg \beta \rightarrow \neg \alpha))$ ref{A2}
    
    if $\neg \alpha$:
    
        $(\neg \beta \rightarrow \neg \alpha)$ ref{DT1, 6}

        $((\neg \beta \rightarrow \neg \alpha) \rightarrow (\alpha \rightarrow \beta))$ ref{A3}

        $(\alpha \rightarrow \beta)$ ref{MP, 10, 12}
        
    $(\neg \alpha \rightarrow (\alpha \rightarrow \beta))$ ref{DT2, 14}
