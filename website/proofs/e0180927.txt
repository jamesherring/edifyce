
let $\alpha$, $\beta$, $\gamma$ be formula

# First three axioms the same as in propositional logic

$(\alpha \rightarrow (\beta \rightarrow \alpha))$ ref{A1}

$((\alpha \rightarrow (\beta \rightarrow \gamma)) \rightarrow ((\alpha \rightarrow \beta) \rightarrow (\alpha \rightarrow \gamma)))$ ref{A2}

$((\neg \beta \rightarrow \neg \alpha) \rightarrow (\alpha \rightarrow \beta))$ ref{A3}


let $x$, $y$ be variable, $t$ be term


if $t$.is_free_for($x$, $\alpha$):

    $(\forall x \; \alpha \rightarrow \alpha[t/x])$ ref{A4}


if $x$ not in $\alpha$.free_variables:

    $(\forall x \; (\alpha \rightarrow \beta) \rightarrow (\alpha \rightarrow \forall x \; \beta))$ ref{A5}


$\forall x \; x = x$ ref{A6}


let $\alpha$, $\beta$ be atomic_formula

if $\alpha$.replace_equivalent($\beta$, $x$, $y$):

    $(x = y \rightarrow (\alpha \rightarrow \beta))$ ref{A7}
